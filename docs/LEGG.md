# In acknowledgment of Timothy Legg

The verse-level transcription and proofing of the Cherokee New Testament
archived in this repository is the work of **Timothy Legg**. He initiated the
"Cherokee New Testament Online" project in 2006 to compare the English and
Cherokee New Testament verse by verse, digitized the printed American Bible
Society edition on a flatbed scanner, and transcribed the entire New
Testament by hand in the vi text editor on Ubuntu Linux over the course of
about three years — followed by two complete proofreading passes. The project
ran from 2006 to 2019 on cherokeenewtestament.org; its verse-level scans and
transcriptions are the direct basis of this repository's `cntdata/` data,
seeded from his own 2011 data archive.

His work has made the 1860 Cherokee New Testament machine-readable —
searchable verse by verse, in syllabary and Latin transliteration, side by
side with the English — and freely available to Cherokee language learners,
researchers, and software projects. Independent projects have built on it:
the CrossWire Bible Society distributes it as the SWORD module Che1860, and
the 2015 print/PDF edition credits him directly.

This repository exists because of that labor.

## The project, in his own account

From the project history he wrote (preserved in the
[CherokeeNewTestament.org](https://github.com/CherokeeLanguage/CherokeeNewTestament.org)
repository, `content/cherokee-new-testament/index.md`):

- The printed New Testament was given to him by Ryan Mackey while he was
  staying in Kenwood, Oklahoma; the book's glue melted in a hot car and the
  loose pages were placed on a flatbed scanner.
- The late George Pumpkin suggested comparing the English and Cherokee
  documents.
- The English verse-per-line text came from the University of Virginia — the
  only source parsing every verse onto its own line, which made the encoding
  workable.
- An audio project with Louise Lacey and Betty Sixkiller recorded a single
  session; it remains on hold.
- In November 2014, Michael Conrad created a PDF e-book of the text.
